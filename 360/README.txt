HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN 360 PHOTOS
1. Put the item on a turntable or walk around it, taking 24 to 30 photos at equal steps, same height and distance.
2. Name them 01.jpg, 02.jpg, 03.jpg ... in order (landscape 4:3, about 1600 px wide, under 250 KB each).
3. Put them in a folder named after the item, for example 360/garland/ (replace the sample frames).
4. In 360/manifest.json set "count" to the number of photos. To add an item, add a new entry and a new folder.
